Facebook Marketplace software for car dealers

AutoLander is a native desktop application, paired with a cloud service, that car dealerships and independent sales reps use to merchandise inventory on Facebook Marketplace. Connect the inventory source you already run — CarGurus, Cars.com, a DMS export from vAuto, DealerCenter, Frazer, CDK, Tekion or HomeNet, an SFTP or CSV drop, or your own dealer website — and AutoLander builds a posting queue from it, prepares each listing with photos, mileage, price and description, and posts through your own logged-in Facebook session at a pace you control.

It then keeps Marketplace matched to the lot: asking prices stay in step with the feed, new arrivals are queued automatically, and sold units are pulled down so buyers stop messaging about cars that are already gone. AI photo editing turns the shots you already have into showroom-grade listing images without repainting the vehicle, RV and camper inventory is posted into the correct Marketplace category, and buyer messages are routed to your team so a human answers them faster.

Plans start at $39 per month — Starter $39, Growth $59, Pro $79, and dealer and multi-rooftop plans from $117. Every demo includes 5 free posts and requires no credit card. AutoLander works inside Meta’s eligibility rules and listing limits rather than around them, and it does not auto-reply to buyers on your behalf.
