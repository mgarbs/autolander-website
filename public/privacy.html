Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 23, 2026

AutoLander LLC (“AutoLander,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides software that helps automotive dealerships automate the listing of their inventory on Facebook Marketplace and other platforms. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, how it is disclosed, and the security practices we follow to protect it.

This policy applies to our website at autolander.ai, the AutoLander desktop application, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, and dealership name when you sign up or request a demo.
  • Billing information: payment is processed by our payment provider, Stripe. Stripe collects and stores your card details directly; AutoLander does not see or store full card numbers. We retain only limited transaction metadata (e.g., subscription tier, billing status, last four digits, billing email) returned by Stripe.
  • Support communications: messages you send to our support team and any information you choose to include.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage telemetry and error logs: diagnostic information about how the desktop app and website are used (feature usage, performance metrics, crash reports, error stack traces, app version, operating system) so we can fix bugs and improve the Service.
  • Website analytics: standard log data such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and pages visited.

1.3 Inventory and vehicle data

  • Inventory feed data: when you connect a CarGurus or Cars.com feed, we retrieve vehicle data from that feed (VIN, year, make, model, trim, price, mileage, photos, descriptions, specs) so we can generate listings on your behalf.
  • Vehicle photos: photos pulled from your inventory feed are sent to third-party GPU servers (RunPod) for AI processing such as background removal and studio backdrop compositing. Photos are processed transiently and are not retained long-term on those servers.

1.4 Facebook Marketplace session data

To post listings on your behalf, the AutoLander desktop application uses your active Facebook session on the device where it is installed. Your Facebook credentials and session cookies remain local to your machine and are never transmitted to or stored on AutoLander servers. AutoLander does not collect, log, or have access to your Facebook password.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including syncing your inventory and posting listings on your behalf.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions through Stripe.
  • To process vehicle photos through our AI pipeline.
  • To diagnose, fix, and prevent bugs, errors, and abuse using telemetry and error logs.
  • To communicate with you about your account, billing, product updates, and customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. How We Share and Disclose Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below:

  • Service providers (sub-processors): we share information with vendors who perform services on our behalf, under contractual confidentiality and security obligations. These currently include:
    • Stripe — payment processing.
    • RunPod — GPU compute for AI photo processing (vehicle images only; processed transiently).
    • Cloud hosting, email, and analytics providers used to operate the Service.
  • Inventory and listing platforms: when you instruct AutoLander to publish a listing, the listing content (photos, description, price, vehicle details) is transmitted to the destination platform (e.g., Facebook Marketplace) from your device.
  • Legal and safety: we may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
  • Business transfers: if AutoLander is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

3.1 Method of disclosure

When we share information with sub-processors, transmission occurs over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS) using API integrations or secure file transfer. Service providers act on our behalf under written agreements that restrict use of the information to the services they provide to us.

4. Security Practices

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. These include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Encryption at rest for stored account and billing metadata.
  • Access controls limiting employee access to production systems on a need-to-know basis.
  • Use of reputable infrastructure providers with industry-standard security certifications.
  • Storage of Facebook session data locally on your device (not on AutoLander servers), reducing the attack surface for credential exposure.
  • Logging and monitoring to detect anomalous activity.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work continuously to improve our practices.

5. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Telemetry and error logs are retained for a limited period (typically up to 12 months) for debugging and product improvement. Vehicle photos sent to GPU servers for AI processing are not retained long-term. Billing records are retained as required by law and for legitimate accounting purposes.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access, correction, deletion: you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information by emailing privacy@autolander.ai.
  • Account closure: you may close your account at any time. We will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period after closure, except where retention is required by law.
  • Marketing communications: you may opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in any such email. Transactional messages (billing, security, service updates) cannot be opted out of while you have an active account.

7. SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number to AutoLander — including when booking a demo, signing up for our Service, or communicating with our team — you may receive SMS text messages from us related to:

  • Appointment confirmations, reminders, and rescheduling
  • Account and service notifications
  • Customer support communications
  • Onboarding and account setup

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories of personal information described in this Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with us. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS communications. Opting out of SMS will not affect your ability to receive email or in-app communications from us.

By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive text messages from AutoLander at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

8. Children

The Service is intended for use by automotive dealerships and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Users

AutoLander is operated from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those of your country.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Effective date” at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email.
