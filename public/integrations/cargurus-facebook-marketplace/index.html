Can AutoLander post my CarGurus inventory to Facebook Marketplace?

Yes. CarGurus is a directly supported feed source, so AutoLander can post your CarGurus inventory to Facebook Marketplace automatically. AutoLander reads your CarGurus inventory feed — year, make, model, price, mileage, photos and descriptions — and publishes each vehicle to Marketplace, then keeps the listings current as your lot changes.

Because AutoLander runs as a native desktop app on your own computer, it posts through your normal Facebook session instead of a shared cloud server or a browser extension.
