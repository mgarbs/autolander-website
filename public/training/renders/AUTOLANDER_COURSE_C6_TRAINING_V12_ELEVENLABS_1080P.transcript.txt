First-day readiness and your next practice

Let's finish with the tasks you'll use, not just the lessons you've watched. Open your workbook and mark each relevant task as independent, needs coaching, blocked by setup or access, or not your role.

For a salesperson, check that you can use the right account, confirm Facebook and inventory, select a vehicle, read the Queue, review listing changes and answer a buyer's question. If you use Auto-Pilot, explain what needs to stay running and where you check its state.

For an individual account owner, include feed setup, listing wording and the difference between your posting allowance and AI credits. For a manager, include team access, the correct reporting period and one practical coaching task. A manager-only user doesn't need to publish a vehicle to complete their training.

Pick one task you want to improve and replay that lesson. Product Tour is at the bottom left of the AutoLander sidebar if you need help finding the screen. Then try a different example and see whether you can explain and locate the step without help.

If a setup issue is still open, write down who is helping and what happens next. You don't need to buy, post or delete something just to finish the workbook. Bring the same checks into your next working day, and build confidence one successful task at a time.
