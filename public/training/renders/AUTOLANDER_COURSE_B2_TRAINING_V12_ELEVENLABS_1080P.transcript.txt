Write the words buyers should see

Click Configuration in the sidebar, then open Post Style. This is where you shape the wording that goes with your vehicle information.

Start with Dealer Playbook. Look at the example in the app, then replace it with clear information your dealership actually wants buyers to read. Managers and admins set the shared dealership wording, so agree on it before changing it for the team.

For example: Financing available. Trade-ins welcome. We speak Spanish. Use only statements your store has approved and services it offers. Add the dealership's approved fee wording where appropriate; don't copy another store's fees into your own listings. Check for spelling mistakes and repeated lines before saving your changes.

My Listing Additions is your closing information at the bottom of the description. You could write: Please message me for more pictures and information. My phone number is in my bio. Then include your approved contact email.

If you mention your phone number in your bio, add it to your Facebook profile as well and check that shoppers can see it. Listing Additions doesn't update your profile for you.

Now look at the complete finished listing. The vehicle features and optional emojis come first, followed by dealership information and the closing contact line. This example also shows fee wording. Read the whole description after posting to make sure it says what you intended.

For practice, draft one shared dealership line and one personal closing line. Read them together and remove any duplication.
