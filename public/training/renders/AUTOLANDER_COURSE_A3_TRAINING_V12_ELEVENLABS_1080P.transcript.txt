Select the right vehicle and post

Click Post to Market in the left sidebar, then choose Available. First, make sure these are vehicles from the dealership and location you intend to advertise. If the inventory looks wrong, don't start a post. On a dealer account, tell your manager. On an individual account, contact the AutoLander admin through Support.

Use the search box or the filters above the vehicles to find the car you want. Check its identity, price and mileage against the dealership's information. The photos come from your inventory source; an AI option doesn't correct missing or inaccurate source details.

Click the selection box on a vehicle card. You can select more than one, and the Post to Marketplace button shows how many you've chosen. Keep the cards and that button in view while you check the selection.

Leave AI Background off to use the original photos, or choose an optional treatment after checking its displayed credit cost. We'll cover those choices in the AI lesson.

When you're ready for an intended post, click Post to Marketplace. The work moves to the Queue. For this practice, select one suitable vehicle and explain your checks. You can stop before the posting button.
