Choose and check an AI background

AI Background is optional. It changes the setting around a vehicle from your inventory photo. This is the original image beside the finished result for the same vehicle.

To choose a style, click Configuration in the left sidebar. Open Post Style, then find the background choices. Select the style you want to use.

Now click Post to Market in the sidebar. On the vehicle card, find AI Background. First Photo changes the lead image. The other options cover more exterior photos.

Before starting the post, read the credit cost beside your choice. Prefer the original photos? Leave AI Background off.

You'll see the generated result after posting. Open the finished Marketplace listing and check that the image still represents the vehicle accurately. If something is wrong, have the listing corrected. Dealer-account users can tell their manager; individual users can contact the AutoLander admin.

For this exercise, find the style and First Photo controls without starting a post. Then compare the original and finished photos shown here. Your workbook has space to note the option and its cost.
