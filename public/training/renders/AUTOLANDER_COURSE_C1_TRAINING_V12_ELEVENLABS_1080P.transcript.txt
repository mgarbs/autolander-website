Manual selection or Auto-Pilot

Use manual selection when you have particular vehicles in mind. Choose them in Post to Market and start that batch. Use Auto-Pilot for the ongoing routine.

Let's find its settings. Click Post to Market in the left sidebar. Find Auto-Pilot, then choose Configure.

Set the start and stop times for your posting window. Choose which inventory to use, then your photo options. Read the message about the window and your account's daily allowance.

When the settings are right, turn Auto-Pilot on. Check that it shows an active state. It spreads posting across your chosen window, so you don't have to start each batch yourself.

It also keeps existing listings up to date. When a price changes in your connected inventory, Auto-Pilot can update the matching Facebook listing posted through AutoLander. When a vehicle is marked sold, it handles the supported sold update too. You don't need to sit on the Listing Updates page and watch.

Keep the computer powered on and connected to the internet, with AutoLander running. You can minimize the app. Closing it or shutting down the computer stops that work. On a laptop, make sure the computer stays awake.

Check the app each day, and again after a restart or lost connection. Confirm Facebook, the feed and Auto-Pilot, then read any unfinished Queue items.

Which mode fits your day? Note that in your workbook, then find the schedule on your own screen. Change the live settings only when you're ready to use them.
