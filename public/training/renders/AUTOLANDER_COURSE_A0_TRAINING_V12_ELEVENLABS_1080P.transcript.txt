Let's get AutoLander ready on your Windows or Mac laptop or desktop. Open autolander dot ai. At the top right, next to Book a Demo, choose Download.

On Windows, you may see a security prompt. First, confirm the installer came from AutoLander dot A I. If your browser blocks the download, open Downloads and choose Keep only for that trusted file. Choose Show in Folder, or Open Containing Folder, then open the installer. If Windows says, Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway, if offered and allowed by your organization. The next screens are normal setup. Choose Only for me unless your I T team says otherwise, then Next. Keep the default folder and click Install. When it finishes, leave Run AutoLander checked and click Finish. These Windows steps do not apply on Mac. If a malware alert or company policy blocks installation, stop and ask for help.

Once the app opens, you'll see this login screen. If your dealership invited you, use the details in your AutoLander email. Check your spam folder if it's missing, then ask your manager. This is your AutoLander login, not your Facebook password. We'll connect Facebook separately after you're signed in. Keep both passwords private. Moving to another computer? Sign in to your existing account. Forgot password is below Sign In. If you're setting up your own account for the first time, choose Register below Sign In and follow the instructions.
