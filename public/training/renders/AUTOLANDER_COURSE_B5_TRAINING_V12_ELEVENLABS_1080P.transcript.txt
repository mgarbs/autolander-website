Posting allowance and optional credits

Your subscription's posting allowance and your optional AI credits do different jobs. Let's find both.

If you manage billing, click Configuration in the sidebar, then Billing. Review the plan and current terms for your account. The Pro plan's current allowance is up to fifteen posts a day. Other plans and team arrangements can differ, so use the allowance that belongs to your account.

In Post to Market, check the posts remaining today before choosing a batch. That remaining number is not the same as your AI credit balance.

AI Background options show their credit cost alongside the choice. Leaving the option off uses the original photos. Choosing a treatment uses the applicable credits. A credit pack adds AI credits; it doesn't automatically raise your plan's daily posting allowance.

If you want to purchase credits or change plans, review the current amount and terms in the app before confirming. There's no purchase required for this lesson. On a dealer account, ask your manager about billing or limits. On an individual account, contact the AutoLander admin if the plan information needs clarification.
