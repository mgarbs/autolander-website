Find your way around

Start on Sales Hub. This is your overview. Read the labels beside the numbers so you know whether you're looking at inventory, posting activity or something that needs attention.

The main navigation stays on the left. Move down to Post to Market and click it. This is where you'll find your vehicles. Available, Queue and Posted to FB sit across the top of the working area. They separate vehicles you can choose, work in progress, and listings you've posted.

Next, click Listing Updates in the left sidebar. This page brings together price changes and vehicles that are no longer in the connected inventory.

Now click Configuration. The tabs here include Facebook, Inventory Feeds and Post Style. You'll use these for your connections and listing settings. Other tabs depend on your access.

Product Tour is at the bottom left, and Support is also in the sidebar. Pause the video and find those four destinations yourself: Post to Market, Listing Updates, Configuration and Product Tour. You don't need to change anything.
