Use the dashboard to coach the next task

Click Manager Dashboard in the left sidebar. Start with the reporting period. Choose Today, This Week or This Month. Use the same period when comparing activity.

Read the label on each number. Posting activity, active listings and sold reporting tell you different things. A reported sale is useful context, but that number alone doesn't explain what led to it.

If a teammate's activity looks low, start with a simple question. What happened with your last post? Ask them to open Post to Market and show you the Queue. If the task stopped, check the connection and inventory together.

Can't find the page? Go to Configuration, then Team, and check their access. If the setup is ready but a step is unclear, replay that lesson. Let the teammate show you the step afterwards.

Agree on one useful next action, such as checking a failed row or confirming the correct inventory. Follow up on that action during the next shift.

In your workbook, choose a reporting period and one metric. Write the question you would ask, and the next task you would check together.
