Use relevant groups and refresh carefully

For groups, start in Configuration and click My Groups. With Facebook connected, choose Load From Facebook to see the groups available from your account. Select the relevant local buy-and-sell groups you want to use, then choose Save Groups.

Pick groups that fit your area and the vehicles you sell. Read their rules. Group administrators still decide what gets approved. Your choices don't guarantee that a post will appear in that group. You can come back here to review your choices.

For an older Marketplace listing, first check that the vehicle is still available and its information is current. Then click Post to Market in the sidebar and choose Posted to FB, next to Available and Queue.

Find the vehicle and choose Repost. The blue Delete and Repost control appears. This removes the original listing published through AutoLander and creates a fresh listing for that vehicle. It isn't simply an edit to the old description, and it doesn't guarantee a particular place in a shopper's feed.

For practice, locate your group settings and the Posted to FB tab. Explain what Delete and Repost does, without deleting or reposting a vehicle just for the lesson.
