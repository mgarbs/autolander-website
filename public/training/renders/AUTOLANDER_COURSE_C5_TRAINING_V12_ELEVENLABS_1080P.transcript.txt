Daily routine and better buyer conversations

Start each day with a quick check in AutoLander. Confirm Facebook is connected, your inventory is right, and any unfinished Queue items make sense. If you're using Auto-Pilot, check that it's on and the app can keep running. If you work manually, choose the vehicles you want to post and review Listing Updates.

Then check Facebook Messenger. That's where shopper conversations come to you. AutoLander handles the posting routine; you handle the conversation. Open Messenger on your computer or phone, and keep notifications on so you notice new questions.

Before you finish your shift, check remaining work and any messages you promise to answer.
