A Facebook Marketplace assistant is software that takes the repetitive Marketplace work off a salesperson’s plate — listing every vehicle, keeping prices and details current, enhancing the photos, writing the descriptions, removing sold units, and helping route buyer messages back to your team. Think of it as a tireless assistant dedicated to your Marketplace presence.
