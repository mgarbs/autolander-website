What is a Facebook autoposter?

A Facebook autoposter (or auto poster) is software that automatically publishes listings to Facebook instead of a person posting each one by hand. For car dealers, the listings go to Facebook Marketplace — the part of Facebook where local buyers actually browse for vehicles — so an autoposter reads your inventory and posts every car for you, then keeps the listings current.
